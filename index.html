Students, independent learners and people working on Physics, data analysis, machine-learning or research-related projects. We can discuss your current level and goals before planning a session.

Can I learn online?

Contact me to discuss remote tutoring or project guidance, your preferred schedule and the format that suits your learning goals.

Yes. Services cover Python-based data preparation, exploratory analysis, modelling, evaluation and explaining the workflow behind the results.
